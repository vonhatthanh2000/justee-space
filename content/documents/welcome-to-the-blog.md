# Welcome to the blog

I wanted a place where writing could stay useful after the moment it was published. This blog is a collection of documents rather than a reverse chronological feed. Each document can point toward another idea, and those links gradually form a map.

The subjects begin with the things already shaping my work: [learning in public](/blog/learning-in-public), [building useful AI systems](/blog/building-useful-ai-systems), and finding the small details that make software or creative work feel considered.

## Two ways to explore

Document View is for reading with focus. Graph View is for noticing relationships. Neither is the correct starting point. Follow the path that matches your curiosity.

The graph is explained in [why this blog has a graph](/blog/why-this-blog-has-a-graph).
