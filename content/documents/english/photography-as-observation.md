# Photography as observation

Photography teaches a useful kind of patience. A frame becomes more interesting when I stop searching for a subject and start noticing relationships: light against material, movement against stillness, and one object changing the balance of another.

That habit travels back into engineering. [Learning in public](/blog/learning-in-public) depends on noticing what actually happened, while [finding database bottlenecks](/blog/finding-database-bottlenecks) depends on looking past the most obvious explanation.

## Attention before equipment

Tools matter, but they cannot decide what deserves attention. The first task is learning to see the structure that is already present.
