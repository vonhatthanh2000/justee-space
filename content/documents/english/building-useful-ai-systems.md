# Building useful AI systems

An AI feature becomes useful when it improves a real decision or removes a real obstacle. Starting with the model usually produces a demonstration. Starting with the user and the failure modes has a better chance of producing a system.

I look for three things: the decision being supported, the evidence available to support it, and the feedback that reveals when the system is wrong.

## Build for inspection

Outputs should be inspectable. Sources, uncertainty, and system boundaries need a visible place in the experience. The same principle appears in [finding database bottlenecks](/blog/finding-database-bottlenecks): good systems make their behavior legible.

Writing about those decisions is part of [learning in public](/blog/learning-in-public).
